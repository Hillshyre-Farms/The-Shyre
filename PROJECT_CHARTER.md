# The Shyre Project Charter

## Mission

The Shyre is the permanent digital home of the Rocky Mountain Players Club.

Its purpose is to preserve the complete history of the league, provide live league intelligence through Yahoo Fantasy Football integration, and deliver AI-powered insights while maintaining the highest possible standards of historical accuracy and data integrity.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Data Integrity First
Unknown data remains unknown until verified.

### 2. Every Fact Has Evidence
Every important fact must be traceable to an original source.

Examples include:

- Yahoo Fantasy API
- Commissioner emails
- Tie-out spreadsheets
- League documents
- Manual commissioner verification

### 3. Never Delete History
Corrections never erase history.
Historical changes are logged and traceable.

### 4. Calculated Statistics Are Never Stored
Championship counts, career records, power rankings, and analytics are calculated from verified underlying data.

### 5. Build for the Next 20 Years
Every design decision should support the long-term preservation of the Rocky Mountain Players Club.
